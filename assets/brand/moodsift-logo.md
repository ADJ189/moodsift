# Moodsift brand assets

The wordmark and app icon are a "mood dial": a soft aperture/iris around a
match spark, drawn to stay legible at 16 px and to read as a tool rather than a
streaming-service logo.

| `moodsift-logo-transparent.png` | Master with transparency, for dark or coloured surfaces |

## Rules

- Keep clear space of at least the height of the "M" on every side.
- Do not add gradients, drop shadows, outlines or extra text to the mark.
- On light surfaces use the dark/accent form; on dark surfaces use the
  transparent light form. Do not recolour it to a palette accent.
- The interface name is **Moodsift**, one word, capital M.
- The brand accent is the violet/cyan pair used for data marks (match meters),
  never as gradient headline text.
